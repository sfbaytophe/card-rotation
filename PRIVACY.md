# Privacy

Card Rotation is a planning tool. It does not connect to bank accounts, card networks or any payment service.

## What the app stores

- Family members' first names, as you type them.
- For each card: the bank, card name, network, an optional last 4 digits, who uses it, notes you add, and balance-transfer dates.
- Which cards were used in which month.

The app never asks for, and you should never enter, a full card number, expiry date, security code or login.

## Where it is stored

Everything is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. Nothing is sent to the app's author or to any server. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, so keep a backup (Settings → Back up or move your data).

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, and, only when you import an Excel file, a spreadsheet reader from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. Those services receive ordinary web requests (such as your IP address), not your card data.
