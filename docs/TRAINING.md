# Card Rotation: training guide

How to set up the app, bring in your history, and pick the right cards every month. Screenshots use a sample family, Alex and Sam.

## The monthly rule

| | Default | Where to change it |
|---|---|---|
| Cards per person each month | 1 Amex + 1 Visa | Settings |
| Longest a card can go unused | 3 months, then it must be used in month 4 | Settings |
| 3rd card | Added when needed, any network | Settings (Add when needed / Every month) |

Cards on a balance transfer (BT) or paused are never suggested or listed as due.

---

## 1. Getting started

### Step 1: your family

![Setup step 1](screenshots/01-setup-family.png)

1. Open the app. Setup starts on its own the first time.
2. Tap how many family members will use the cards.
3. Type each person's first name and tap **Next**.

### Step 2: your monthly rule

![Setup step 2](screenshots/02-setup-rule.png)

1. Choose how many Amex and Visa cards each person uses.
2. Choose the longest a card can go unused.
3. Tick the box if everyone gets a 3rd card every month.
4. Tap **Finish setup**.

### Bring in your cards and history

![Get started options](screenshots/03-get-started.png)

Pick the option that matches what you already have: an **Excel or CSV file**, **Paste text** copied from a sheet, or **Just add my cards** (no history). An import also adds any cards it finds.

### Check the import before saving

| Paste or choose a file | Review, then tap Import |
|---|---|
| ![Paste text](screenshots/04-import-paste.png) | ![Import preview](screenshots/05-import-preview.png) |

Nothing is saved until you tap **Import**. Uses already in the history are skipped, so importing the same sheet twice is safe.

## 2. Every month

### This month

![This month](screenshots/06-this-month.png)

- Each person has an **Amex** and a **Visa** slot.
- Faded cards with a dashed outline are **suggestions**. Tap one to use it, or tap **Use 4 suggestions**.
- The arrows switch months.

### Pick another card

![Pick another](screenshots/07-pick-another.png)

1. Tap **Pick another** under a slot.
2. Cards are listed least recently used first.
3. Tick **Include … cards** to borrow another person's card.
4. Tap a card to place it.

### Use every card that's due

![Due list](screenshots/09-due.png)

- **Due in [month]** lists cards that have gone unused for 3 months.
- Some are already suggested for a slot. For the rest, tap **Add for [name]**.
- A red note means more cards are due than there are slots.

### Add a 3rd card when more are due

![Month filled in with a 3rd card](screenshots/08-month-filled.png)

Tap **Add a 3rd card this month** under a person. The app suggests the most overdue card. Tap **No 3rd card** to remove the slot.

## 3. Wallet

![Wallet](screenshots/10-wallet.png)

- Filter by bank or search by name, last 4 digits or notes.
- Each card shows when it was last used and the month it must be used by.
- **Add card** or **Edit** to change a card's details. Only the last 4 digits are stored.

![Edit card](screenshots/11-edit-card.png)

### Update when a card was last used

Used a card outside the plan, or is a date wrong? Record it here. Suggestions and due dates update right away.

**One card:** tap **Last used** on the card's row.

![Last used for one card](screenshots/12-last-used.png)

1. Choose the month and who used it.
2. Tap **Record use**.
3. To correct a mistake, remove the wrong month with the **×**. The most recent month left becomes the last used date.

**Many cards:** tap **Update last used** at the top of the Wallet.

![Update last used for many cards](screenshots/13-update-last-used.png)

1. Fill in a month (and who) only for the cards that changed. Blank rows are left as they are.
2. Tap **Save**.

## 4. History

![History](screenshots/14-history.png)

One row per month, newest first, with a column per person and slot. **Other cards used** lists extras and due cards. Tap a month to open it.

## 5. BT & Paused

![BT and Paused](screenshots/15-bt-paused.png)

- Every card has a toggle: **On**, **BT** or **Paused**.
- **BT** means the card carries a balance transfer. Set when it started and when the promo ends.
- **Paused** means set aside, for example for a phone bill. Add a reason.
- **Mark paid off** records the payoff month and puts the card back in the rotation.

## 6. Settings

![Settings](screenshots/16-settings.png)

Change family members, cards per person, months unused, the 3rd card option and which cards can fill a Visa slot. **Your data** has Import past usage, Run setup again, and Back up or move your data.

---

## Monthly routine (about five minutes on the 1st)

1. **Open This month** and check the new month's suggestions.
2. **Accept or swap** them.
3. **Clear the due list**: add every due card as a 3rd card or extra.
4. **Update BT & Paused**: mark payoffs and new transfers.

If plans change mid-month, update the slots or record the card under **Wallet → Last used**.

## Common questions

**Someone has no Amex of their own?** The app borrows the family's least-used Amex and labels it as another person's card.

**Mastercard in a Visa slot?** In Settings, set "Which cards can fill a Visa slot" to **Any non-Amex card**.

**Imported the same sheet twice?** No harm done. Uses already in the history are skipped.

**Is my card number stored?** No. Only the card name and its last 4 digits. Never enter a full number.
